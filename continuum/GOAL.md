# Quorum — goal

A funding body runs research calls end to end in one place, and every outcome can be explained:

- researchers submit proposals to an open call before its deadline;
- program officers screen them, with an AI triage agent that only advises;
- three independent, verified reviewers score each eligible proposal without seeing each other's work first;
- a panel chair decides, and awards can never exceed the call's budget;
- oversight sees the whole portfolio and an audit trail of who did what, when and why.

Success feels like this:
- an applicant always knows where their proposal stands;
- a reviewer cannot be steered by a peer's score;
- a chair cannot overspend, even with two awards racing;
- a refused action says which rule refused it;
- a crash in the middle of processing loses nothing and duplicates nothing.

The MVP runs on one machine with no paid infrastructure. ORCID, the triage model and outbound email are stubbed behind their declared boundaries.

The Goal explains intent. The semantic ADL defines architectural
meaning. If they conflict, the semantic model governs.
